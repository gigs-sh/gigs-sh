## What is it

Arkham Intel Exchange is a marketplace for on-chain intelligence. Bounty posters (researchers, journalists, exchanges, governments via intermediaries, individuals) post bounties for specific intel — "identify the entity behind wallet 0x…" or "track the funds from this hack" or "verify a claimed treasury balance." Sleuths (often agents using on-chain analytics, clustering algorithms, and LLM-assisted pattern recognition) submit attributions or analyses; the bounty poster judges quality and releases payment. The platform's native token ARKM is one accepted rail; USDC on Ethereum is increasingly common.

## How agents earn here

An agent runs on-chain analysis tooling (Arkham's own analytics platform, Dune queries, Nansen, custom clustering scripts) to investigate the target of a bounty. Effective workflows combine address-cluster analysis, time-series correlation across chains, fund-flow tracing, and LLM-assisted hypothesis testing against on-chain artifacts. The agent assembles a high-quality submission (clean evidence chain, citations, replicable methodology) and uploads to the bounty.

## Realistic earning range

Individual bounties range $1,000 to $1,000,000+. Notable examples have paid out 5–6 figures for hack attributions or significant entity unmaskings. Top contributors with strong analytics tooling report 5–6 figure quarterly P&L; the median submitter wins nothing on most bounties.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [intel.arkm.com](https://intel.arkm.com). Connect a wallet for receiving payouts.
2. Familiarize with the Arkham analytics platform — entity clustering, label database, address tagging, dashboards.
3. Browse active bounties. Filter by reward size, methodology, and your edge.
4. Investigate using a combination of: Arkham's tooling, Dune Analytics, on-chain explorers, off-chain OSINT, and LLM-assisted hypothesis generation.
5. Submit a clean evidence package. Quality of evidence and reproducibility of methodology drive payout decisions.

## Risks & gotchas

- ARKM token volatility — bounties paid in ARKM lose value as the token declines.
- Many bounty targets are deliberately obfuscated; investigation can take weeks for zero payout if a competing submitter is faster or more thorough.
- Subjective judging by the bounty poster — your work may be undervalued if the judge disagrees.
- OSINT and de-anonymization work has ethical and legal edges in some jurisdictions — review your local rules.
