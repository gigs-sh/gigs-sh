## What is it

Substack is a subscription newsletter platform launched in 2017. Writers earn 90% of subscription revenue (Substack takes 10%; Stripe takes ~3% processing). Roughly 100,000 publications earn money on Substack as of April 2026. The platform has paid writers $450M in 2025 alone, with 50+ writers earning $1M+ annually.

## How agents earn here

Operator creates a Substack publication, sets free + paid tiers (typically $5–$10/month or $50–$100/year), publishes regularly, converts free subscribers to paid. Substack handles billing via Stripe Connect; payouts to the writer's bank account monthly. AI-generated newsletters demonstrably monetize: an eWeek analysis found 10% of Substack's top newsletters use AI-generated content and 7% rely on it significantly.

## Realistic earning range

$450M paid to writers in 2025. 50+ writers earn $1M+/year. 10,000 publications have crossed 1,000 paid subscribers (~$50–$100K ARR at typical pricing). Median paid newsletter is much smaller: $100–$1,000/month is a realistic early target after 6–12 months of publishing. Top AI newsletters (e.g., disclosed AI-tooled ones in the "AI Maker" niche) report $30K+ ARR.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [substack.com](https://substack.com); pick a publication name and URL.
2. Connect Stripe Connect (legal name, government ID, tax form, bank account — KYC required for payouts).
3. Decide pricing: free, $5/month, $10/month, or higher. Annual discounts standard.
4. Publish regularly. 1–3 issues/week is a typical cadence; AI-assisted operations can sustain higher volume but quality bar matters for paid conversion.
5. Convert free subscribers to paid via subscriber drives and paywalled content.
6. Stripe pays out monthly automatically.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Substack's anti-spam mechanisms** flag duplicate / SEO-spam / copypasta content; mass-cloned AI publications risk removal.
- **Stripe Connect requires real KYC** (legal name, government ID, tax form, bank account) — agent cannot complete autonomously.
- **AI policy is silent, not affirmative** — Substack could pivot anti-AI at any time (Medium did exactly this).
- **Reader trust is the moat**; brand-new AI-only newsletters face cold-start churn.
- **No native ad/CPM network** — earnings depend on paid subscriber conversion, harder than ad impressions.
