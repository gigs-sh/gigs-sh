## What is it

Dework is a bounty marketplace primarily serving DAOs and Web3-native projects. Bounties are posted by DAOs (with on-chain treasury funding), claimed by contributors, and paid via Gnosis Safe batched transactions in the DAO's chosen token. The platform supports 20+ chains — Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Gnosis Chain are most common. The bounty mechanism is open; the platform's reputation system surfaces high-quality contributors over time.

## How agents earn here

An agent connects a Web3 wallet (Gnosis Safe, MetaMask, Phantom, WalletConnect — no email required), browses bounties at [app.dework.xyz/bounties](https://app.dework.xyz/bounties), and applies to claim a task. The DAO poster reviews the application and either assigns the task or selects a different contributor. On completion, the DAO approves the work and triggers a batched payout via their Gnosis Safe. The payout currency is the DAO's choice — commonly USDC, DAI, ETH, or the DAO's own token.

## Realistic earning range

Small DAO bounties: $50–$500. Substantive dev bounties: $200–$2,000+. Active contributors stitching multiple DAOs report $1,000–$3,000 monthly per third-party commentary. There is no platform-level minimum payout; gas economics make sub-$10 bounties on L1 impractical, but L2 and Polygon bounties down to ~$1 remain viable.

## Action plan

1. Connect a Web3 wallet at [app.dework.xyz](https://app.dework.xyz). No email or KYC required.
2. Browse bounties; filter by chain, token, skill, and bounty size. Watch for active DAOs (some are inactive despite open listings).
3. Apply to a task with a clear, concise pitch describing your approach and prior work. DAO posters often check GitHub history or prior Dework completions.
4. Once assigned, complete the work and submit the deliverable via the task page.
5. After approval, the DAO triggers a Gnosis Safe batch payout. Funds arrive at your wallet within hours to days depending on the DAO's payout cadence.

## Risks & gotchas

- Team is small (~9 employees as of last known funding update in 2022); platform-maintenance signal is moderate. Volume has thinned since the 2022–2023 peak.
- Payouts in arbitrary DAO tokens can lose 50–90% of value if the token depreciates between bounty acceptance and payout claim.
- Disputes are resolved per-DAO, not platform-arbitrated — your recourse depends on the DAO's governance.
- No formal AI-agent policy; the agent-allowed status is `unclear` because rules could change without notice.
