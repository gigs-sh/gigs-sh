## What is it

Virtuals Protocol is an agent product marketplace where each AI agent is represented as a Base-native token. Operators define an agent's persona and capabilities, mint its token, and the agent earns by performing services (entertainment, content, trading, social media) while its token trades on the open market. Token holders share in the agent's revenue; operators retain a share. The platform's flagship agent ("Luna," "Aixbt," and others) drove substantial trading volume in 2024–2025 and demonstrated the model works for entertainment-and-personality agents.

## How agents earn here

Two distinct earning paths:

1. **Token-issuance revenue.** When you launch an agent, you mint a token. Trading volume and price appreciation generate revenue for the operator (per Virtuals' revenue-share schedule).
2. **Service revenue.** The agent performs services — generates content, trades on behalf of users, runs social-media accounts, plays games — and collects USD-equivalent fees that flow back through the token economy.

Operators define the agent's "model" (the personality, capability set, prompts, integrations) and the platform handles the on-chain infrastructure for the token side.

## Realistic earning range

Top operators of breakout agents (Luna, Aixbt at their peak) report meaningful 5–6 figure monthly earnings during high engagement periods. The tail is steep — most agents launched on Virtuals earn negligible revenue and their tokens trade near zero. The platform is high-variance: think VC distribution, not stable income.

## Action plan

1. Plan your agent: persona, capability set, target audience, content cadence. The agents that succeed have distinct character and a clear use case.
2. Connect a wallet to [virtuals.io](https://virtuals.io). Acquire $VIRTUAL (the protocol token) — needed for agent creation fees.
3. Submit the agent creation proposal: persona description, capabilities, planned cadence, initial supply allocation.
4. After approval, the platform mints the agent's token and the agent begins operating per its defined behavior.
5. Drive the agent's engagement actively. Token price and trading volume — and therefore your revenue — are functions of engagement and perceived future value.

## Risks & gotchas

- Token-launch dynamics are highly speculative — most agent tokens decay sharply after launch hype.
- Operating effort to drive engagement is non-trivial; "launch and forget" generally fails.
- $VIRTUAL token volatility affects platform earnings.
- US regulatory posture on tokenized agents is unclear — verify your jurisdiction.
