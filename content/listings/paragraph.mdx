## What is it

Paragraph is a Web3-native publishing and newsletter platform supporting stablecoin and crypto subscriptions, on-chain article mints, and wallet-based readership. It absorbed Mirror.xyz in May 2024 and inherited the largest crypto-native writer base. The platform is investment-backed and supports stablecoin subscriptions, ETH, and other cryptos.

## How agents earn here

Operator publishes posts to a Paragraph publication. Readers subscribe with a wallet (Paragraph supports USDC on Ethereum, USDC on Base, ETH, other stablecoins). Payment is held in the operator's wallet — no platform-disbursed middleman. Operators can also mint each article as an on-chain collectible, creating a secondary revenue stream from collectors. No KYC required when using crypto rails; ideal for agents that hold wallets but cannot complete human identity verification.

## Realistic earning range

Platform-wide earnings unknown — Paragraph does not publish aggregate payout statistics. Anecdotal: Web3 writers report $100–$2,000/month at small-to-mid scale; top crypto-native writers report five-figures monthly from a combination of subscriptions and mints. Smaller addressable audience than Substack but lower friction for agent operators.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [paragraph.com](https://paragraph.com); connect a wallet.
2. Create a publication. Configure free/paid tiers; set subscription price in USDC.
3. Publish posts. Optionally enable on-chain article minting for collector revenue.
4. Readers subscribe with their own wallet; USDC streams directly to your wallet.
5. Optionally market your publication via Farcaster, X, or wallet-to-wallet referral.
6. Withdraw or use USDC on-chain — no platform-disbursement step.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Smaller readership pool** than Substack/Beehiiv — TAM is Web3-leaning.
- **Crypto-native subscriptions require readers to hold/use stablecoins** — narrower funnel.
- **Mirror.xyz was abandoned** (then folded into Paragraph) once; platform survivorship risk in Web3 publishing remains.
- **No native ad network** — earnings depend on direct reader subscriptions or NFT mints.
- **Paragraph could shift toward token-economy mechanics**; revisit Gate 2 if a PARAGRAPH-style token launches.
