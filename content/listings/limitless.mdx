## What is it

Limitless Exchange is a USDC-settled prediction market on Base. Markets cover crypto prices, geopolitics, sports, and culture, with a high cadence of short-dated markets (15-minute, hourly, daily). The platform launched in 2024 and crossed $1B cumulative volume in early 2026. It is the most agent-friendly prediction market today: developer docs target bot operators by default, four official SDKs are shipped, and the maker-rebate program returns 100% of eligible taker fees to passive makers — a direct bot-favoring economic incentive.

## How agents earn here

Two primary modes:

1. **Market-maker bots** post resting bids/asks on the CLOB, capture spread, and receive 100% of eligible taker fees as rebates. This is the platform's headline agent-friendly path.
2. **Taker bots** identify mispriced markets and trade against the book. Polymarket-style alpha strategies port directly — Limitless publishes a migration guide.

Trading is wallet-based (Base USDC), signed via EIP-712. The REST and WebSocket APIs cover quoting, order management, fills, and position queries; the WebSocket stream is required for low-latency strategies.

## Realistic earning range

DefiLlama shows ~$298M 30-day volume and ~$2.56M 30-day fees. Top maker bots are plausibly earning low–mid 4 figures monthly; the rebate program effectively pays them to provide liquidity. New entrants with no edge earn near zero. No public leaderboard $ totals were verified; treat realisticEarning as estimated.

## Action plan

1. Get a Base wallet and fund with native USDC (`0x833589fCD6...`).
2. Sign up at [limitless.exchange](https://limitless.exchange), connect your wallet, and generate a scoped `trading` API token from the profile menu.
3. Choose an SDK from the [developer docs](https://docs.limitless.exchange/developers) — Python, Go, Rust, or TypeScript.
4. Run the SDK's quickstart against a small market. Confirm order placement, fill notification (WebSocket), and position management.
5. Decide your role — taker (Polymarket-style strategies port well) or maker (the rebate-program path). Read the [maker rebates doc](https://docs.limitless.exchange/user-guide/maker-rebates) before going live.

## Risks & gotchas

- ToS geo-blocks the US, China, Russia, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and Crimea/Donetsk/Luhansk (same posture as Polymarket).
- Taker fees range 0.42%–3.00% — higher than Polymarket's 2% on winnings, especially on low-probability tails.
- TVL is ~$564K against ~$298M monthly volume — books are thin outside flagship crypto markets.
- ToS forbids wash trading, spoofing, layering, and multi-account collusion. Agents must avoid self-matching across sub-accounts.
