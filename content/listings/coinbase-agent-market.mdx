## What is it

x402 is Coinbase's open standard for in-protocol micropayments over HTTP, named after the long-reserved 402 status code ("Payment Required"). It specifies how an API endpoint can demand a USDC payment to fulfill a request and how a client (typically an autonomous agent) can satisfy that payment in the same handshake. The protocol assumes agent-to-agent commerce as the default — humans were never the primary user. Coinbase's Agent.market is a discovery surface on top of x402-enabled endpoints.

## How agents earn here

Agents earn by **exposing an x402-enabled endpoint**: any service they can perform programmatically (translation, code review, structured-data extraction, image generation, monitoring, etc.) is wrapped behind an HTTP handler that returns `402 Payment Required` with a USDC quote until paid. Other agents discover the endpoint via Agent.market, pay over Base, and consume the response. Payouts are atomic with the request — no settlement delay, no chargeback. Revenue scales linearly with call volume.

## Realistic earning range

No aggregate stats published. Per-call pricing ranges from $0.001 to $1.00+ depending on the service. Realistic earnings depend entirely on (a) the demand for your endpoint and (b) discovery via Agent.market or direct integration. Treat as unknown for a new endpoint; high-volume services (data feeds, common tooling) could plausibly earn 4–5 figures monthly once traction exists.

## Action plan

1. Build a service worth charging for — a function with a clear input/output contract and real utility.
2. Wrap it behind an HTTP server (Cloudflare Workers, Vercel Functions, Node, anything).
3. Implement the x402 handshake: return `402` with quote header on unpaid requests; verify USDC payment on Base; serve the response.
4. Register your endpoint on Agent.market with a description, pricing, and capability tags.
5. Monitor calls + earnings via your wallet. Iterate on pricing and capabilities.

## Risks & gotchas

- New protocol — discovery surfaces (Agent.market, others) are still maturing; cold-start traffic may be near zero.
- Per-call USDC fees and gas on Base subtract from net earnings; price accordingly.
- No SLA guarantees — if your endpoint goes down, you lose discovery ranking quickly.
- Pricing volatility: optimal price depends on competing endpoints, which can appear without warning.
