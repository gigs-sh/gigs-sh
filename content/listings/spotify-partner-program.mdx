## What is it

Spotify Partner Program (formerly Spotify for Podcasters) is Spotify's podcast monetization program. Eligible shows earn 50% of ad revenue served against free-tier listens, plus a share of YouTube-style Premium video revenue when uploading video podcasts. Spotify lowered the threshold on January 7, 2026 to 1,000 listeners + 2,000 listening hours + 3 episodes in the prior 30 days (down from the previous 2,000 + 10,000 + 12).

## How agents earn here

Operator hosts a podcast (audio or video), distributes via Spotify, hits the SPP threshold, and earns 50% of ad revenue served against free-tier listens. If video, Premium-tier subscribers also generate revenue (ad-free Premium video share). AI-narrated podcasts (e.g., daily news summaries, NotebookLM-style auto-generated shows) monetize normally — no mandatory disclosure. Payouts via direct deposit to the linked bank account.

## Realistic earning range

Per public creator self-reports: small podcasts $50–$500/month at 5–20K monthly listens; mid-tier $500–$5,000/month at 50–200K listens; top tier $10K–$100K+/month at 1M+ listens. Spotify does not publish program-wide payout totals; numbers from creator-reported income and ad-share research.

## Action plan

1. Set up a Spotify Creators account at [creators.spotify.com](https://creators.spotify.com).
2. Upload your podcast (audio or video). AI-narrated formats are accepted; voice cloning of recognizable real people requires documented consent.
3. Grow to the SPP threshold: 1,000 listeners + 2,000 listening hours + 3 episodes in the prior 30 days.
4. Enable monetization through Spotify Creators dashboard.
5. Set up bank payout method; KYC required for identity + tax verification.
6. Spotify runs monthly payouts once balance exceeds the $10 threshold.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Spotify's Platform Rules** prohibit "audio that consists primarily of non-speech noises" and disproportionate sponsorship loads — mass-produced low-effort AI podcasts risk removal.
- **Voice cloning of recognizable real-person voices requires documented consent** — agent operators cannot legitimately clone a real person without permission.
- **KYC required for monetization onboarding** (banking, tax forms, identity verification).
- **Podcast discoverability is harder than YouTube**; cold start can be slow.
- **Spotify could mirror YouTube's "inauthentic content" crackdown** for podcasts at any time (no current ban, but the door is open).
