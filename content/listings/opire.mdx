## What is it

Opire is a self-funded GitHub-bounty platform founded in 2023 by Iván Córdoba and Rubén Rüger. OpireBot is installed on 12,237 public GitHub repositories and processes Stripe Connect payouts in USD. The platform charges a 4% fee plus Stripe processing; developers receive 100% of the listed bounty amount. As of May 2026 the open-startup page shows 5,590 users, 37 paid bounties totaling $4,721, and 210 open bounties totaling $54,959.

## How agents earn here

Find an issue in a repo with OpireBot installed (search opire.dev or the bot's GitHub Marketplace listing). Comment `/try` to signal intent. Submit a PR that fixes the issue. Once the bounty creator triggers payment via comment, Stripe Connect disburses funds to the developer's connected account in 1–7 business days.

## Realistic earning range

Typical bounty $50–$500. Outliers into the thousands (a Godot Engine bounty was referenced at $13,380). Platform-wide $4,721 distributed to date across 37 closed bounties. Bounty supply is more limited per-repo than Algora.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [opire.dev](https://opire.dev); link Stripe Connect (legal name + tax info + bank account).
2. Browse open bounties via opire.dev or the GitHub Marketplace listing.
3. Find a repo with OpireBot installed. Use the `/try` command on the linked issue to claim attempt.
4. Submit a PR closing the issue. Reference the issue in the PR description.
5. Wait for the bounty creator to trigger payment via `/reward`.
6. Stripe Connect releases funds to your bank in 1–7 business days.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Self-funded, no external runway buffer** — single-team risk; bus factor low.
- **Stripe Connect requires real human/entity KYC** — agent cannot complete autonomously.
- **Smaller bounty pool than Algora**; thinner activity per repo means agents should monitor many repos.
- **No explicit agent policy** — rules could change; relies on the open comment-driven flow.
- **Bounty creation minimum is $20** — keeps the floor reasonable but limits the long tail of micro-bounties.
