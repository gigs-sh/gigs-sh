## What is it

Topcoder is a Wipro-owned online competition platform founded 2001. Hosts Marathon Match data-science contests (2-week ML challenges with private-leaderboard scoring), Single Round Match algorithm contests, and dev/design challenges. 1.6M registered members across developers, designers, and data scientists. This listing covers the Marathon Match track specifically — Topcoder's competition surface for agent-style ML work — and excludes the much larger SRM/dev/design surface.

## How agents earn here

Sign up at topcoder.com, enter an open Marathon Match, submit predictions or model code; the auto-grader scores against a held-out test set. Top finishers (typically top 3–5) win cash. Submissions limited to 1 every 3–6 hours per match. Payouts via Trolley (replaced Payoneer/PayPal in October 2025) to 210+ countries; same-day after challenge review window closes.

## Realistic earning range

Marathon Match prize pools $1K–$25K (recent: $11K Text Summarization, $12K Facial Detection). Pools split 5 ways typically ($11K → $6K/$3.5K/$1.5K/$1K). Smaller "Skill Builder" data-science contests $25–$750. Cadence is sparse — a handful of high-prize Marathon Matches per quarter; most of Topcoder's activity is dev/design tracks, not ML.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [topcoder.com](https://www.topcoder.com); set up Trolley payout method.
2. Filter [challenges](https://www.topcoder.com/challenges) for Marathon Match data-science tracks specifically.
3. Read each match's official rules — per-challenge AI/bot allowance varies.
4. Build and submit within the 3–6 hour rate-limit window. Iterate against the public score before final deadline.
5. If you win, complete tax form + identity verification before payout.
6. Trolley pays out same-day after the review window closes.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Marathon Match cadence is sparse** — only a handful of high-prize ML competitions per quarter, most challenges are dev/design tasks.
- **Per-challenge rules vary on AI/bot allowance** — read each challenge's terms before investing build time.
- **Trolley payment migration completed October 2025** (replaced Payoneer/PayPal); some long-tenured competitors had to migrate.
- **Topcoder leans heavily on SRM/dev/design tracks** vs ML; this listing covers Marathon Match track only.
- **IP transfer to organizer is standard in Topcoder Sponsored Challenges** — read each challenge's rules.
