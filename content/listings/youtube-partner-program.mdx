## What is it

YouTube Partner Program is YouTube's monetization program. Eligible channels earn a 55% share of pre-roll/mid-roll ad revenue, a share of the YouTube Shorts ad pool, a share of YouTube Premium revenue based on watch time, plus channel memberships, Super Thanks, and Shopping. YouTube paid creators $32B+ in 2024 (over $100B since 2021) across 3M+ partner channels.

## How agents earn here

Operator creates a YouTube channel and uploads videos (AI-narrated, AI-generated visuals, faceless format all fit). Channel hits the YPP threshold (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days). Monetization is enabled after policy review. YouTube serves ads against the channel's videos and pays 55% of long-form ad revenue and a Shorts pool share via AdSense monthly, $100 minimum threshold.

## Realistic earning range

Faceless AI channels report wide variance: small ($100–$1K/month at 50K views/mo), mid-tier ($1K–$10K/month at 500K–2M views/mo), top tier ($10K–$45K+/month at 5M+ views/mo). RPM averages ~$2–$8 for AI niches (versus $15–$30 for human-led education / finance). YouTube total payouts: $32B in 2024.

## Action plan

1. Create a Google account + YouTube channel; verify with phone.
2. Upload AI-generated videos. Disclose synthetic/altered content via the "Altered content" toggle when prompted — this is required by policy when content appears realistic.
3. Grow to the YPP threshold (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days). Typical ramp: 6–18 months for new AI channels.
4. Apply to YPP via YouTube Studio → Monetization. Pass the policy review (channel-wide, retroactive).
5. Set up AdSense (legal name, tax forms, bank account). KYC required at this step.
6. AdSense pays monthly when the balance crosses $100 USD via direct deposit / wire / check depending on country.

## Risks & gotchas

- **AI-disclosure required** for realistic synthetic content; failure to disclose risks penalties up to YPP suspension.
- **"Inauthentic content" policy** (renamed from "repetitious content" July 15, 2025) demonetized thousands of low-effort AI farms — quality bar matters.
- **AdSense requires real human KYC** (legal name, tax forms, bank account) — agent cannot complete this autonomously.
- **Channel-wide enforcement** — one bad video can pull monetization across the entire channel.
- **Slow ramp** — hitting the 1,000-sub/4,000-hour threshold typically takes 6–18 months for new AI channels.
