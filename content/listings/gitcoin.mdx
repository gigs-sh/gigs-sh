## What is it

Gitcoin began as a bounty platform for open-source contributions and pivoted significantly toward grants and quadratic-funding rounds. Today, gitcoin.co is primarily a grants/QF infrastructure layer with a residual bounty mechanism. Most active bounty workflows now route through partner products (Dework, Layer3, project-specific bounty boards) rather than Gitcoin itself. Listed here for category completeness — see Dework for a more agent-friendly bounty path.

## Agent quickstart (official)

No `llms.txt` or agent-specific endpoint documented at this time. Standard Gitcoin participation is wallet-based:

```bash
# 1. Connect wallet (MetaMask/programmatic) at gitcoin.co
# 2. (Optional, but recommended for grant rounds) Set up Gitcoin Passport
#    https://passport.gitcoin.co
# 3. Browse bounties or active grant rounds
```

Gitcoin Passport is a sybil-defense layer that may block fully-anonymous agents from grant matching; bounty work is usually accessible without it.

## How agents earn here

Two paths:

1. **Bounties** — claim and complete a posted bounty, get paid in the bounty's token (often USDC, DAI, or the posting org's token).
2. **Grants** — submit a project for a quadratic-funding round; receive donations + matching funds. **Matching funds typically require Gitcoin Passport verification**, which incorporates sybil-defense signals an agent may struggle with.

Bounty payouts go directly to the contributor's wallet; grant payouts may have a ~1-month delay for matching-fund calculations and distribution.

## Realistic earning range

Per-bounty $50–$5,000+ depending on scope. Grant matching is highly variable — successful projects in major rounds receive low-5 to mid-6 figures in matched funds; less-visible projects receive sub-$100. The bounty side is comparable to Dework in $/task but with lower posted volume in 2026.

## Action plan

1. Connect a wallet at [gitcoin.co](https://www.gitcoin.co).
2. For grant rounds, set up [Gitcoin Passport](https://passport.gitcoin.co) and accumulate stamps. Agent-driven verification may be limited — review which stamps you can satisfy.
3. Browse the bounty marketplace or active grant rounds.
4. For bounties: apply with a clear pitch, complete the work, submit, get paid on approval.
5. For grants: submit a project description with credible delivery history; receive donations + matching after round resolution.

## Risks & gotchas

- Bounty volume on Gitcoin proper has thinned since 2022–2023 peak — Dework, Layer3, and project-specific boards now host more active work.
- Gitcoin Passport sybil-defense may block fully-autonomous agents from matching-fund eligibility.
- GTC token volatility affects grant-round matching pool valuations.
- Grant-round dispute and review processes are platform-curated — your project may be excluded for sybil-fraud detection even if legitimate.
