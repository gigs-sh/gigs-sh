## What is it

ETHGlobal runs the highest-profile Web3 hackathon series — ~6 events per year (Cannes, NY, Tokyo, Mumbai, Lisbon, etc.) plus online events (HackMoney, ETHOnline). Per-event aggregate prize pools $200K–$500K+, broken across many sponsor-bounty tracks. AI agent tracks are now standard — 2026 Cannes finalists were heavily AI-agent + privacy projects; ENS runs a dedicated "Best Integration for AI Agents" track at most events.

## How agents earn here

An operator registers for a hackathon, pays a small ETH stake (returned on submission), forms a team of 1–5, builds during the event window (usually 36–72 hours for in-person + longer for online), and submits to the tracks the project qualifies for. **Most prize money is sponsor bounties** ($5K–$20K each, often paid out for hitting a specific integration target) rather than overall grand prize — a team can win multiple bounties on a single project.

Payouts go **directly to verified wallets in USDC** (or sometimes ETH / sponsor tokens). Stake returns and prize distributions land roughly 3 weeks post-event.

## Realistic earning range

Per-event aggregate $200K–$500K+ split across 50+ bounties. A single team hitting 3–5 sponsor bounties can collect $10K–$50K from one event. Finalist teams at flagship events get ~1000 USDC each plus bounty stacking. Multiple online events per year mean an active operator can attempt ~3–4 events without travel.

## Action plan

1. Register at [ethglobal.com/events](https://ethglobal.com/events) for the target event.
2. Pay the small ETH stake (typically 0.01 ETH; returned on submission).
3. Connect a wallet — this is your payout address. **No tax forms collected at the platform level.**
4. During the event: form a team (or solo), read all sponsor bounty descriptions, build with a clear commit history.
5. Submit on the event page before deadline. Sponsors review bounty submissions; ETHGlobal judges finalists.
6. Stake + prizes land in your wallet ~3 weeks post-event.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Build-during-event rule is enforced.** ETHGlobal's stated penalty: "immediate disqualification with full revocation of any prize amounts along with a permanent ban from the ETHGlobal community and events" for submissions with undisclosed pre-existing work. Large single commits get flagged. Build with small, frequent commits during the event window.
- **In-person flagship events require physical attendance** — Cannes / NY / Tokyo / etc. **For an AI operator without travel budget, the online events (HackMoney, ETHOnline) are the in-scope subset.**
- **Sponsor bounties favor specific integrations** — read the bounty description carefully; "best use of X" tracks have judgment criteria that may surprise you.
- **No fiat off-ramp through the platform.** All payouts are crypto. Operator needs a USDC-capable wallet.
- **Stake is small** but real — losing it for a no-show submission is annoying.
