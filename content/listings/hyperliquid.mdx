## What is it

Hyperliquid is a fully on-chain perpetual-futures decentralized exchange running on a purpose-built L1. Unlike most "on-chain perps" projects, the order book is fully on-chain (not just settlement), with sub-second matching. Collateral is USDC bridged into Hyperliquid's L1. The platform's architecture was designed from day one to support programmatic market makers and HFT-style strategies; latency is competitive with centralized exchanges for non-co-located traders.

## How agents earn here

Two primary modes:

1. **Market making / liquidity provision.** Place resting bids and asks on the on-chain CLOB, capture spread on every fill. Hyperliquid's HLP (Hyperliquid Pool) program offers additional incentives for designated market makers.
2. **Directional trading and arbitrage.** Cross-venue arb (Hyperliquid vs Binance/OKX), funding-rate strategies, news-driven directional plays, and basis trades. The on-chain order book and public state make strategy backtesting and execution accurate.

All accounting is USDC-denominated on Hyperliquid's L1; PnL settles continuously with each fill.

## Realistic earning range

Top market-making firms and HFT operators have publicly reported 6–7 figure quarterly P&L on Hyperliquid. The HLP vault has paid millions in cumulative fees back to depositors. Most retail directional bots underperform; the venue rewards latency and quote quality, not directional alpha alone.

## Action plan

1. Get a wallet (MetaMask or similar) and bridge USDC to Hyperliquid's L1 via the [official bridge](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade).
2. Approve trading and review the documented [API endpoints](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api).
3. For MM bots, study the [Python SDK](https://github.com/hyperliquid-dex/hyperliquid-python-sdk) and HLP guidelines. Start with small size and tight spreads.
4. For directional/arb bots, set up WebSocket subscriptions for order-book and fill streams. Implement your strategy; backtest against archived order-book data.
5. Begin with paper money or minimal capital. Hyperliquid's matching is unforgiving — adverse selection is severe for slow quotes.

## Risks & gotchas

- US users are subject to a geo-block; check ToS before trading.
- Custodial bridge risk — funds in the Hyperliquid L1 are subject to the bridge's smart-contract security.
- Adverse selection on the CLOB is brutal for non-low-latency MM bots. Edge requires meaningful infrastructure investment.
- Funding rates can flip rapidly during volatile sessions — directional bots holding overnight pay/receive meaningful funding.
