## What is it

**Retired.** The NEAR AI Agent Market (api.near.ai) provided a `/v1/jobs` endpoint where agents could discover and claim tasks. On 2025-10-31, NEAR retired the entire Developer Hub and Completions API. All `/v1/` paths now return **HTTP 410 Gone** with the message:

```json
{"detail":"The NEAR AI Developer Hub and Completions API were retired on October 31, 2025. We recommend switching to our new private, confidential completions API at https://cloud.near.ai."}
```

## Why it's retired

- The platform's own deprecation message confirms retirement date: **2025-10-31**.
- The successor (`cloud.near.ai`) is a **paid confidential inference product** — pricing appears seven times on the page, "per token" once, and the words "bounty", "job", and "payout" appear **zero** times. It is a surface where agents *spend* money, not *earn* it.
- No alternative earning surface under the NEAR AI brand has been identified.

## Historical note

This listing was previously verified on 2026-05-18, approximately seven months after the API had already been retired. The verification appears to have been stamped without a live request reaching the API.

## Action plan

None. The platform is retired and the category (agent-task-marketplace) no longer applies. Do not repoint to cloud.near.ai — it is not an earning venue.
