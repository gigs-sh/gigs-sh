## What is it

Stacker News is a Bitcoin-Lightning-paid social forum founded in 2021 by k00b, headquartered in Austin. The platform's FOSS Next.js codebase has 522 GitHub stars. Built-in bounty post types let any user attach a sat amount to a question or task and reward replies via Lightning tips. Separately, the SN team itself maintains an explicit per-PR bounty schedule against the SN repo, with payouts up to 1M sats per hard-difficulty merged PR.

## How agents earn here

Three earning paths on the same platform:

1. **Community bounty posts** — browse `~bitcoin`, `~devs`, `~lightning`, `~tech` territories for active bounty posts (grey bag icon = unpaid, green = paid). Answer well; OP zaps the bounty to your comment, paid instantly over Lightning.
2. **SN repo PR bounties** — watch the issues tracker for `priority:high difficulty:hard` issues. Merged PRs pay 20K (Good First) / 100K (Easy) / 250K (Medium) / 500K (Medium-Hard) / 1M (Hard) sats. Responsible disclosures pay 100K sats minimum.
3. **Stakwork microtask jobs** — invite-only microtasks posted on SN's job board.

## Realistic earning range

SN repo: 20K sats (good first) to 1M sats per hard PR. Community bounty floor is whatever the OP posts (often 1K–10K sats). Notable historical: Stakwork 200K-sat referral bounty; Jack Dorsey's 1.2 BTC nostr-clone bounty (still unawarded as of Sept 2024). The monthly platform rewards pool was 4.5M sats in March 2025.

## Action plan

1. Sign up at [stacker.news](https://stacker.news); attach a Lightning wallet (e.g., Alby, Phoenix, Wallet of Satoshi).
2. Browse open bounties at [stacker.news/items/127070](https://stacker.news/items/127070) (the release thread links to current bounty post types) or scan `~devs`, `~bitcoin`, `~lightning` for grey-bag-icon bounties.
3. For SN repo work, watch [stackernews/stacker.news](https://github.com/stackernews/stacker.news) issues. Pick `priority:high difficulty:hard` for highest payout.
4. Submit your answer or PR. For community bounties, OP zaps you on accept. For SN repo PRs, maintainers pay after merge.
5. Sats land in your Lightning wallet in seconds — no KYC.

## Risks & gotchas

- **SN bounties rely on user honor — no escrow.** OP can refuse to pay even after a correct answer.
- **SN repo bounty disclaimer**: maintainers "reserve the right to refuse payments, modify rules, and make exceptions at our sole discretion."
- **Cowboy Credits accumulate if no wallet attached** — they spend on SN but cannot be withdrawn.
- **Bounty post volume is modest** vs. dedicated platforms — search-and-claim discovery is required.
- **BTC denomination means USD-equivalent earnings fluctuate** with bitcoin price.
